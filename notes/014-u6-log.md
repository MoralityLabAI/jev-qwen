## What U6 shows

1. **The Jev's repair gain transfers when it is trained on the target failures.**
   - Trained on real proposals, it fixes 68% of standard-size real failures, against 52% for
     dual_repair and 33% for c_repair.
   - It fixes 11% at 8x8, where neither projection fixes any.
   - Trained only on synthetic perturbations, it fixed 17% (U5).
2. **Real-failure training teaches both learners to refuse.** It cuts the Jev's broken commits from
   74% to 11%.
3. **Specialising has a cost, in both directions.**
   - Campsite training cost J-multi about 2.4 points on S4, probably about 2 on S1.
   - Real-failure training cost J-u4 12-19 points on the synthetic defects.
4. **The CSP solver still solves every proposal** at every size in milliseconds. On Campsite the
   learned repairers are a research question, not a practical need.
