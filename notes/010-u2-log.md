## Where the pipelines lose items (POST HOC diagnostics, not registered)

- **Gate.** Every Jev and generic-Qwen gate error is a zero-delta, non-exact state whose rule label
  is reject; those gates commit it. In the S2 near-miss training split the 77 zero-delta rows are
  labelled consistently (46 exact -> commit, 31 non-exact -> reject). TRM-cv and kNN learned that
  boundary; the text gates did not carry it to the new states. This alone explains why C trails A.
- **Routing, joint items.**
  - J-multi mostly fails by abstaining (75 of 360 at seed 0), plausibly learned from the S7
    training negatives.
  - J-V0 sends them to the sibling logic skill (48) or the math skill (30).
  - Route accuracy on joint items is similar (0.68 vs 0.70).
  - J-multi is better on confusable (0.964 vs 0.907) and negative items (0.915 vs 0.833).
- **TRM router.**
  - It fails all 90 negatives. hermes-lite's `trm_typed` has no abstain option, and the negative
    requests name the forbidden skill, so it lands in the shortlist and gets chosen. In registered
    S7, negatives passed mostly because the forbidden contract was not in the shortlist (v1 log XC6).
  - It misroutes Campsite requests to mcp-trm-retrieval-optimization (52) and
    primehub-structured-map (50).
  - The ticket frame drops its joint route accuracy to 0.33.
  - Its 0.992 on registered S7 does not transfer to these synthetic requests. That is a limit of
    the router on new phrasing, and of templated requests as a test of it.

## Answer to the question, as far as U2 can give it

A small Jev doing both steps does **not** beat a generic small Qwen that picks the skill and lets
the TRM gate decide (T1 not supported; the Jev pipeline is slightly lower). It does beat a generic
Qwen doing both steps (T3).
- **Gate:** the TRM gate is better than either text gate on the commit/veto rule (1.000 vs about
  0.95).
- **Routing:** the Jev is the better router on confusable and negative requests.
- **Best combination tested:** Jev routing with the TRM gate (H, 0.787).
- **TRM router:** on these synthetic requests it is the weakest router, with no abstain path and
  poor handling of new phrasing. On registered S7 it remains near perfect.
