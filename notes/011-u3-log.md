  rudder picks the repair action and the commit action from the pre-repair state.

**What this shows**
- **Model scale does not matter here; retrieval does.** The published lift from 0.36-0.49 (raw) to
  0.795 (with retrieval) comes from retrieval that leaks the answer. A 4B base model with the same
  retrieval reaches the same 0.795, and so do the Jev and the looped Jev.
- **Without the leak, three different arms tie at 34/54 on the holdout rows:** the generic Qwen, the
  repair TRM and a train-majority lookup table. Each is right on every attainable row except the two
  holdout c_signature_fail rejects, which it commits. The TRM and the lookup commit every
  c_signature_fail row.
- **The Jev trades the other way.**
  - It always picks the right repair action in-family.
  - It rejects all four outcome-dependent c_signature_fail rows (two in val_seen, two in
    holdout_seen) on every seed. No other leak-free arm does: J-V0 gets one, kNN two, the TRM and
    the lookup none.
  - It also rejects successful c_signature_fail repairs: 9, 11 and 6 false rejects over the three
    seeds in-family, and no false commit there.
  - Overall unsafe rates (88 rows, including the unseen family): Jev 0.045, J-V0 0.068, Repair-TRM
    0.087, lookup 0.114.
  - So it scores lower on joint accuracy because it is conservative, not because it lets bad
    repairs through.
- **The looped Jev varies by seed.** Seed 1 rejects 15 good repairs; seed 2 misses only the two
  c_signature_fail rejects.
- **Action-space** (MeTTa fixes the repair, the arm only commits or rejects; descriptive): the Jev
  0.826, 27B 0.807, the looped Jev 0.780, then 3B, 9B and J-V0 at 0.750. 0.750 is the commit base
  rate: those three commit on every row.
- **Static-gate:** uninformative here (addendum U3-A1). The rule at Hermes-Skills HEAD decides every
  row.

**Answer to your question, from this benchmark.**
- There is no trained TRM repair module to compare against, so I trained the planned one.
- **Repair TRM:** on the repair decision it ties a lookup table and the generic small Qwen.
- **Jev:** a little lower on joint accuracy (R2 not significant). It is the only arm that vetoes
  every outcome-dependent bad repair, and it pays for that with false rejects.
- **The benchmark:** it mostly measures label lookup plus whether retrieval leaks the answer. With
  four outcome-dependent rows, it cannot separate these arms on repair skill.
