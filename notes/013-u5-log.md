## Design probe: re-solving never becomes the expensive step

Probes on throwaway seeds, before registration:
- **Solver:** hermes-lite's CSP takes milliseconds up to 12x12 (at most 399 nodes).
- **Projections:** c_repair needs 15-78 s per call at 12x12, and dual_repair more than 60 s already
  at 10x10.
- **Generator:** planting a solvable puzzle did not finish in 60 s at 14x14 or 16x16, 6 of 6 tries.

So with these tools the projections become impractical long before re-solving does. Part P
therefore uses 8x8 and 10x10, runs dual_repair with a 60 s cap at 8x8, and skips it at 10x10.

**Reading so far.**

  projections usually change the grid without fixing it, so the unchanged-grid test rarely fires.

### Real proposals (474)

- **Deciding.** J-u4 scores 0.240 (seeds 0.255 / 0.255 / 0.209) against 0.191 for the
  Decision-TRM, but it almost never rejects (2.3%), so 74% of its items are broken commits.
  - That is about the dual policy's plain mode: 0.257 success, 74% unsafe.
  - By band: std 0.486 success / 0.49 unsafe; 8x8 and 10x10 0 success, 97-99% unsafe.
- **Writing.** J-u4 scores 0.084 against c_repair's 0.160.
  - std: 0.171 vs 0.325 for c_repair and 0.521 for dual_repair; 0 at 8x8 and 10x10.
  - By proposer: J-V0's proposals 0.100, Bonsai's 0.069.
  - About 4.3 s per grid.
- **What it means.** Neither trained model carries what it learned from synthetic defects over to
  real model failures. The fixed projections do better, and only the verifier gate (verify-reject)
  makes any policy safe there.

## What U5 shows

1. **A reject path is cheap and works.**
   - On synthetic defects, the verifier-free noop-reject cuts broken commits from 27% to 2% at no
     loss of success.
   - On real proposals only verify-reject (one verifier call after the repair) does it: 74% to 0%.
   - The patch in `patches/hermes-skills/` adds both.
2. **Trained on the same items, the Jev matches the TRM as decider and beats the repair code as
   repairer,** on the same defect distribution. Untrained it lost both (SPEC-U4).
3. **On real proposals and larger puzzles everything learned degrades.**
   - The projections fix half the std proposals and none at 8x8 or 10x10.
   - The CSP solver fixes 100% in milliseconds at every size these tools can generate.
   - The puzzle where re-solving is expensive could not be built: projections and the generator
     give out first.
